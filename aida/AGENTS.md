# Aida loop publishing rules

- Before publishing any new or replacement loop, measure integrated loudness (LUFS-I) and true peak against the existing published loops, accounting for player gain and mono/stereo playback. Match perceived playback loudness; do not match sample peaks alone.
- The current loops player reference is approximately -16.7 LUFS-I in stereo after player gain. Keep new loops within 0.5 LU of that reference and true peaks at or below -1 dBTP. Prefer constant gain; preserve dynamics and source files. Re-measure the final published audio, including any player gain.
- Verify the loop seam after processing, and confirm the audio, image, duration, and player entry load before completing publication.
